# 30-second demo script

Record the real running app (`streamlit run app.py`). Keep the video at or under
30 seconds. Load the page once before recording so the NASA data are cached and
the screen responds instantly.

| Time | On screen | Say (or caption) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | App title and the source line under it | "EarthScope: explore how Earth's environment is changing, using NASA data." |
| 5–11 s | Sidebar: Bridgetown, Barbados; temperature; 1991–2025; click **Analyze** | "Pick a place, a variable and a range of years." |
| 11–19 s | Chart of annual means with dashed trend lines; hover over a point | "EarthScope plots each year and fits a trend line." |
| 19–26 s | Metric cards and the plain-language sentence, then the comparison table | "It shows how fast it's changing, the uncertainty, and whether the trend is statistically significant, here compared with Kingston." |
| 26–30 s | Download buttons, then scroll back to the title | "Built on NASA POWER data. EarthScope, by team Bajan Chief." |

## Before recording

- [ ] The numbers you read aloud match what is on screen at recording time.
- [ ] No personal information, other tabs or notifications are visible.
- [ ] Final length is 30 seconds or less.
- [ ] The video link opens in a private browser window without signing in.

## If using slides instead (7 maximum)

1. Title: EarthScope, Bajan Chief, NASA Space Apps 2026
2. Problem: trends and significance are hard to read from raw data
3. Data: NASA POWER / MERRA-2, locations and period
4. Solution: screenshots of the running app
5. Method: complete-year annual means and linear regression
6. Results: the computed trends, with caveats
7. Limitations, links and team
